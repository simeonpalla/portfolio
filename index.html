Top 10

ADB AI for Safer Roads Challenge 2026: Speed Safety Score

Three-person team · Jun – Jul 2026 · Maharashtra (India) and Thailand

Are posted speed limits appropriate for the road, rather than just how many drivers break them? We used ADB-provided GPS speed data enriched with population, infrastructure, settlement and street-imagery layers to assess 14,711 road segments.

  • Built a composite Speed Safety Score combining Safe System alignment, speed-limit credibility gap and vulnerable-road-user risk.
  • Designed a Priority Index = Exposure × Likelihood × Severity to rank roads for intervention under resource constraints, using population density, junction density and proximity to schools, hospitals, markets and transit.
  • Grounded findings in the WHO Safe System approach, the Nilsson Power Model and AASHTO guidance.
