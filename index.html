Speed Safety Score: are the limits even right?

Road safety is usually framed around drivers who exceed the limit. We asked a different question: does the posted limit make sense for the road itself? Our three-person team scored road segments across Maharashtra and Thailand from 0 to 100, then ranked where intervention would matter most.

05 · About

Mechanical engineer by training. Data person by practice.

Problem solver by habit.

I started in mechanical engineering, moved into data analytics, and gradually found myself working where technology, public policy and real-world systems meet.

I'm drawn to problems where the data is messy, the question isn't fully defined, and the answer needs to be more than a chart. Sometimes that's a model. Sometimes a dashboard, a policy brief or a product.

I'm less attached to a particular tool than to understanding the problem and building something useful.
